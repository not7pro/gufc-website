Thirty Years of Excellence

Built on Passion,
Proven by Trust

Gulf Union Foods Co. began with a simple but powerful belief: that the Gulf deserved world-class beverages — crafted locally, meeting the highest international standards, and accessible to all.

From our first factory in 1999 to four state-of-the-art production facilities today, every step of our journey has been guided by an unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and the communities we serve.

Four Factories,
One Standard

Our state-of-the-art production facilities collectively produce 500M+ liters per year, operating to the highest international standards.

Our Plants

Plant 1

Spread across 22,000 m², Plant 1 is GUFC's second biggest factory and is operated by a team of highly specialized operators and engineers that are fully committed and dedicated to produce Juice, Soft Drinks, Malt Beverages, and more, in PET, Glass & Cans.

Size: 22,000 m²
Production Lines: 6 Production Lines.
Products:
  • Juices & Drinks
  • Juices with Chunks/Float
  • Soft Drinks
  • Malt Beverages
  • Energy Drinks
Pack Types:
  • PET Bottles
  • Glass Bottles
  • Cans
Our Plants

Plant 2

At 38,000 m², Plant 2 is GUFC's biggest factory, specialized in Sparkling Drinks & Malt Beverages. It's also equipped with a state-of-the-art warehousing facility that can accommodate 2M cases of finished goods.

Size: 38,000 m²
Production Lines: 1 Production Line.
Products:
  • Sparkling Drinks
  • Malt Beverages
Pack Types:
  • Glass Bottles
Our Plants

Sustainability

Our Commitment to
People & Planet

Sustainability is not a strategy — it is embedded in every decision we make, from how we source ingredients to how we treat our team and the communities around us.

🌍
Environmental Stewardship

Certified to ISO 14001 for Environmental Management. We continuously reduce our carbon footprint, optimize water usage, and minimize waste across all production facilities.

👷
People First

Our 1,300+ team members are our greatest asset. Certified to ISO 45001 for Occupational Health & Safety, we provide a safe, dignified, and growth-oriented workplace for all.

🏘️
Community Impact

From job creation across the Kingdom to supporting local suppliers and social initiatives — GUFC is committed to creating value that extends well beyond the bottle.
