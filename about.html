Our
Heritage

Gulf Union Foods Co. (GUFC) is a trusted name in the beverage industry, focused on delivering diverse, high-quality beverages across every category — crafted to suit every consumer, and to share every moment.

Our Foundation

Purpose, Vision &
Values

🎯
Our Mission

To make refreshing moments available to everyone, across all touchpoints, offering products that people love and trust.

🔭
Our Vision

To be the leading, most trusted name in the FMCG sector by providing high-quality beverages that cater to diverse consumer needs — combining innovation, value, and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

🤝
Our Commitment

Craft high-quality, refreshing beverages for every occasion, built on trust, value, and consistency across every product and brand — every day, in every bottle.

World-Class Manufacturing

Four Factories,
One Standard

Our state-of-the-art production facilities operate to the highest international standards, collectively producing 500M+ liters per year.

01
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Jeddah Flagship Plant

Our first and largest facility — operational since 1999 — sets the benchmark for our quality processes. Home to the Original® and KLASSE production lines.

02
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Riyadh Production Center

Expanded capacity serving the central and eastern KSA markets. Specializing in energy drink and sparkling beverage production.

03
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Advanced Packaging Facility

Dedicated packaging and quality control hub ensuring every SKU leaving our facilities meets our exacting standards before reaching market shelves.

04
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
Export & Logistics Plant

Our newest facility, purpose-built for international export operations — enabling the consistent supply chain that reaches 42+ countries worldwide.

Responsibility

Our Commitment to
People & Planet

Sustainability is not a strategy — it is embedded in every decision we make, from how we source ingredients to how we treat our team and the communities around us.

🌍
Environmental Stewardship

Certified to ISO 14001 for Environmental Management. We continuously work to reduce our carbon footprint, optimize water usage, and minimize waste across all production facilities.

👷
People First

Our 1,300+ team members are our greatest asset. Certified to ISO 45001 for Occupational Health & Safety, we provide a safe, dignified, and growth-oriented workplace for all.

🏘️
Community Impact

From job creation across the Kingdom to supporting local suppliers and social initiatives — GUFC is committed to creating value that extends well beyond the bottle.
