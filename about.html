Who We Are

Our Company

Beverages Crafted with
Purpose & Passion

Gulf Union Foods Co. (GUFC) is a trusted name in the beverage industry, focused on delivering diverse, high-quality beverages across various categories. Our portfolio spans juices, energy drinks, non-alcoholic malt, soft drinks, and sparkling juices — crafted to suit every consumer, and to share every moment. Founded in 1999 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, GUFC has grown into a regional powerhouse with a presence spanning over 30 markets across the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.

Our History

Milestones That Shaped Our Legacy

From a humble beginning in 1999 to becoming a regional powerhouse — every year marks a new chapter in our journey.

1999
🚀 Gulf Union Foods Co. Founded

Gulf Union Foods Co. was established in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with a bold vision: to deliver world-class beverages that bring people together. The Original® brand was born, introducing premium fruit juices to the GCC market.

2002
📦 First GCC Distribution Network

GUFC expanded its distribution network across the GCC, reaching consumers in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, and Oman. Original® became a household name for quality fruit juices.

2005
🏭 State-of-the-Art Manufacturing Facility

GUFC inaugurated its advanced manufacturing facility equipped with the latest food-grade technology, significantly increasing production capacity and setting new quality benchmarks in the industry.

2008
🍺 KLASSE Brand Launch

The KLASSE brand was introduced — a premium non-alcoholic malt beverage with authentic German taste, extending GUFC's portfolio beyond fruit juices and capturing a new consumer segment.

2012
🏅 ISO & HACCP Certifications Achieved

GUFC earned ISO 9001 and HACCP certifications, reinforcing our commitment to the highest international food safety and quality management standards. Halal certification was also obtained for all product lines.

2015
⚡ COBRA Energy Drink Launch

COBRA — the bold energy drink brand — was launched, bringing powerful energy and dynamic branding to a rapidly growing consumer segment. "Stronger Than You Imagine" became the rallying call for a new generation.

2018
🌍 Regional Expansion — 30+ Markets

GUFC reached a historic milestone by establishing distribution in over 30 countries across the Middle East, Africa, and Asia. Captain, Splash, Ori, and Al Qobtan brands were all launched during this expansion phase.

2021
🏆 500 Million Litres Annual Production

GUFC crossed the landmark of 500 million litres of annual production, a testament to the immense trust consumers place in our brands. The workforce grew to over 1,500 dedicated employees.

2024
🍹 Original® Chunks — Zero Added Sugar

GUFC launched Original® Chunks — a bold new product line featuring real fruit chunks with zero added sugar. The "Fruit Tornado" concept set new standards for healthy indulgence in the beverage sector.

2026
Present
🌟 Gulfood 2026 & Continued Innovation

GUFC participated in Gulfood 2026 in Dubai — the world's premier food & beverage exhibition — showcasing its expanding portfolio and commitment to building the future of beverages in the region and beyond.

Sustainability

Committed to a Greener Future

GUFC's sustainability strategy is built on three pillars: environmental stewardship, community engagement, and responsible governance.

🌱

Eco-Friendly Packaging

Transitioning to recyclable and reduced-plastic packaging across all product lines, contributing to a circular economy and reducing our environmental footprint.

💧

Water Conservation

Implementing advanced water recycling systems in our manufacturing facilities to minimize waste and reduce our total water consumption by 30% by 2030.

☀️

Renewable Energy

Integrating solar energy solutions into our production facilities, targeting 40% renewable energy usage by 2028 in alignment with Saudi Vision 2030.

🤝

Community Investment

GUFC invests in local communities through educational scholarships, youth employment initiatives, and health awareness programs across all markets we serve.

📊

Responsible Sourcing

Sourcing raw materials from certified, sustainable suppliers and ensuring our entire supply chain upholds ethical labor, environmental, and quality standards.

🏛️

Corporate Governance

Maintaining the highest standards of transparency, accountability, and ethical business conduct across all levels of the organization and our global supply chain.
