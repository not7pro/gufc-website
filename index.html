Our Heritage

Thirty Years of
Crafted Excellence

Gulf Union Foods Co. (GUFC) is a trusted name in the beverage industry, focused on delivering diverse, high-quality beverages across every category. Our portfolio spans juices, energy drinks, non-alcoholic malt, soft drinks, and sparkling juices — crafted to suit every consumer, to share every moment.

Read Our Story
1995
The Foundation Gulf Union Foods Co. is established in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with a bold vision to redefine the regional beverage landscape.
1999
First Factory Doors Open Production begins at our first world-class manufacturing plant, setting new benchmarks for quality in the region.
2005
Original® is Born The launch of Original® — our flagship juice brand — marks a turning point, quickly capturing the hearts of consumers across the Gulf.
2010
Portfolio Expansion KLASSE and COBRA join the family, diversifying into malt beverages and energy drinks. Export markets grow to 25+ countries.
2018
Global Reach Four manufacturing facilities now operating at full capacity. Exports reach 42+ countries across 5 continents.
2026
500 Million Liters & Beyond GUFC now produces over 500M+ liters annually with 1,300+ team members, continuing to innovate and lead the FMCG sector.
