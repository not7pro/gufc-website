Our Heritage

Thirty Years of
Crafted Excellence

Gulf Union Foods Co. (GUFC) was established in 1995 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with a bold vision: to redefine the regional beverage landscape through quality, innovation, and trust. Today, with 500M+ liters produced annually and a presence in 42+ countries, that vision is reality.

Milestones

The Journey

1995
The Foundation

Gulf Union Foods Co. is established in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with a bold vision to redefine the regional beverage landscape.

1999
First Factory Opens

Our first world-class manufacturing plant becomes operational, setting new benchmarks for quality across the region.

2005
Original® is Born

The launch of Original® — our flagship juice brand — quickly captures the hearts of consumers across the Gulf.

2010
Portfolio Expands

KLASSE and COBRA join the family, diversifying into malt beverages and energy drinks. Export markets grow to 25+ countries.

2018
Global Reach

Four manufacturing facilities at full capacity. Exports reach 42+ countries across 5 continents.

2026
500M Liters & Beyond

GUFC now produces 500M+ liters annually with 1,300+ team members, continuing to lead and innovate in the FMCG sector.
