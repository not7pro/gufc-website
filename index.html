Our Company

A Trusted Name in Premium Beverages

Gulf Union Foods Co. (GUFC) is a trusted name in the beverage industry, focused on delivering diverse, high-quality beverages across various categories. Our portfolio spans juices, energy drinks, non-alcoholic malt, soft drinks, and sparkling juices, crafted to suit every consumer and to share every moment.

Founded in 1999 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, GUFC has grown into a regional powerhouse with a presence spanning over 30 markets across the Middle East, Africa, and beyond.
