Brand Story

Where Every Sip
Tells a Story

Original® was born from a single, powerful belief: that every person deserves a drink that truly tastes of the real thing. Sourced from the finest fruits and crafted with meticulous care, each variant in the Original® family is a distinct experience.

From the vibrant freshness of our classic Pet bottles to the bold innovation of our Iced Coffee range — Original® continues to redefine what a beverage can be.
